研究集会について

概要 本研究集会『固体地球変動イベントをとらえる時系列データ解析の最前線』は、地震火山をはじめとする顕著なイベントを記録する時系列データ解析を行う研究者が集い、様々な時系列データの時空間解像度の違いや現象を検知・解析するための手法を共有する。現象により良く迫るために、時系列データを扱う研究者の直面する課題やそれを解決するための方法について議論する研究集会です。
This workshop; Solid-earth Transients Workshop, brings together researchers who analyze time-series geophysical data that record notable events related to, but not limited to, earthquakes and volcanic activity. The workshop aims to share the differences in spatiotemporal resolution across various types of time-series data, as well as methods for detecting and analyzing such phenomena. To better resolve these phenomena, it provides a venue to discuss the challenges that researchers working with time-series data face and the approaches to addressing them.

The format for poster presentations is flexible. Poster boards (portrait orientation, large enough to display A0-size posters) will be provided. Informal presentations and discussions using a laptop or similar are also welcome.
The workshop primarily operates in Japanese; however, we may encourage presenters to provide content, such as slides or posters, in English to accommodate a broader audience.
